Your quotes stay yours.

QuotePilot stores quotes in this browser on this device. The app has no account, server, analytics, advertising, or payment processing.

CSV export creates a file on your device. Printing or saving a quote as PDF is initiated by you. The app never emails quotes automatically. Requesting early access opens GitHub and shares only what you choose to submit there.

Clearing this browser’s site data removes locally saved quotes. Export a CSV first if you want a backup.

Questions: use the Request early access link in the app and include only information you choose to share.
